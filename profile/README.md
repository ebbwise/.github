## Ebbwise

A tracker for people whose chronic conditions ebb and flow: ulcerative
colitis, coeliac disease, liver fibrosis and others that don't move in
straight lines.

It is built for the bad days: logging that works one-handed in two
minutes, wearable data that arrives without typing, and analysis that looks
for what tends to come before a flare up to 72 hours later. It describes
patterns in your own data and never diagnoses or gives treatment advice.

Private preview, UK and EU only.
